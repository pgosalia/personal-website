About

I'm a cybersecurity engineer with more than five years in IT and security operations. For the past two years I've worked in a healthcare environment, where I help detect, investigate, and respond to security incidents and keep the systems people depend on safe.

My day-to-day covers building and tuning detections, configuring endpoint, network, email, and identity protections, and shaping security policy around NIST CSF and HIPAA. I also train colleagues, because good security depends as much on people as on tools.

I care about practical, well-documented work and about protecting the organizations that serve their communities. I'm looking to put these skills toward public-sector and community cyber defense in New Jersey.

I started in IT support and moved into dedicated security engineering. I hold an MS in Data Science and AI and a BS in Computer Information Systems.
